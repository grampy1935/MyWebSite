# Volume Settings

Adjust the playback volume by tapping and dragging the tip of the volume slider left or right.

Double-tap anywhere on the slider (except the tip) to toggle mute on or off.

Double-tap the instrument name to call up the Sound Settings view.
The equalizer allows you to adjust the volume of the low, mid, and high frequencies independently.
If the "USB Keyboard" setting is turned on in the Settings view, you can adjust the volume of the keyboard.
